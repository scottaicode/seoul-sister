## Source 1 — Mario Apuzzo (TikTok Shop affiliate course funnel)

**What it actually is:** a course funnel, not a product video. "$7,000 profit in a single day,"
"zero to millionaires in under 12 months," "infinite money glitch" → free masterclass in bio → paid
program. **Treat every income claim as marketing, not data.**

| Beat | Time | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| Preemptive-controversy hook | 0:00 | "This is gonna piss people off, but I'm just gonna say it" — buys ~2s before any claim |
| Concrete number, not adjective | 0:03 | "more than an entire year of salary" |
| **Objection stack, pre-answered** | 0:15 | "didn't have to become an influencer, didn't invest in inventory, didn't ship" — kills 3 swipe reasons up front |
| Show-don't-tell proof | 1:16 | Cuts to a revenue dashboard |
| **Live method demo** | 1:41 | Walks the actual workflow on camera |
| Free-thing CTA | 2:11 | Asks for a click, not a purchase |

**Ports:** the objection stack (0:15) and the live demo (1:41).
**Doesn't port:** the income-claim register; the 2:33 runtime.

## Source 2 — makeup/skincare TikTok Shop seller ("$20k+/month")

A genuinely teachable 7-beat template, taught live while selling a lip gloss.

| # | Beat | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook — verbal AND visual** | Says something slightly outlandish *while doing a physical action* (applying, removing, clapping). Motion holds the eye while the claim lands. |
| 2 | Social proof | Celebrity name-drop / "everyone is talking about this" |
| 3 | Sell the dream | Name the category's problems, then why this one solves them |
| 4 | **Objection handling — the split** | Separates the **product objection** ("too sticky") from the **decision objection** ("I don't know what shade to get") |
| 5 | Price / urgency | Sale, BOGO, "getting a steal right now" |
| 6 | Social proof again | Personal this time: friends, mom |
| 7 | Soft CTA | *"I try to make it as organic as possible so people don't know I'm trying to sell them"* |

### The one genuinely valuable idea: beat 4's split

The **decision objection** is the quiet conversion killer almost nobody addresses. Her version is
"I don't know what shade to get." Seoul Sister's version is:

> **"I don't know which of 6,000 products is for my skin."**

That is not merely a script beat — it is the product thesis. Yuri exists because *the decision* is
the hard part, not the product. It needs no 7-beat container; it is a 20-second face-to-camera video
on its own.

### Also ports: beat 1's "verbal AND visual"

Cheap, mechanical, effective — **hands should be doing something during every hook**, not sitting
still. Applying, swatching, holding a product up, putting something down.

### ❌ REJECT — beat 7, and not merely as "off-brand"

Her stated intent is to **disguise commercial intent** ("so people don't know I'm trying to sell
them"). Bailey is an **owner-promoter with FTC disclosure obligations** — a material connection must
be disclosed clearly and conspicuously, in-video and above the caption fold. Beat 7 is the precise
inverse of what she is legally required to do. Reject outright; do not soften.

### ❌ REJECT — beat 5 (price/urgency)

There is no BOGO on a $24.99/mo subscription. Manufacturing urgency also undercuts the
buy-less differentiator that a Seoul Sister video leads with three beats earlier.

### ⚠️ CONDITIONAL — beats 2 and 6 (social proof)

Seoul Sister's real social proof is thin and specific (2 paying subscribers, a handful of genuine
cold-stranger conversations, Bailey's own Reddit history, her 450 messages / 51 conversations / 7
corrections). Use **only what is true**. "My mom uses it" is fine if true; "everyone is talking about
it" is not true yet. Non-Negotiable #1 — one fabricated claim breaks the moat, and that costs more
than the beat is worth.

## Summary — what to actually take

| Take | Leave |
|---|---|
| Beat 4 split (decision objection ≠ product objection) | Beat 5 urgency/BOGO |
| Beat 1 hook-with-motion (verbal + visual) | Beat 7 disguised CTA (**FTC problem**) |
| Beat 3 sell-the-dream (name problems → why this solves) | Income-claim register |
| Mario's objection stack + live demo | 2min+ polished runtime |
| | Beats 2/6 unless the proof is literally true |

**The through-line:** take what holds ATTENTION, reject what closes a TRANSACTION. And keep the
format scrappy — `BAILEY-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md` is explicit that phone / face-to-camera / 15-30s /
no editing beats produced content, and both sources above are produced sales content.
