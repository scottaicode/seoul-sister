## The finding that drives everything

**The in-video DEMO is the conversion mechanism.** Not the pitch, not accumulated parasocial trust.

Cal AI — consumer subscription app, same price class — reached ~$2M/mo overwhelmingly via TikTok
organic: ~1,000 videos in 12 months that demo the product doing something impressive and name it.
Viewers converted from **single videos**, never having seen the creator before. Founders fronted
their own content. (Tier B, corroborated across three independent writeups.)

Translation for Bailey: **show Yuri actually doing the thing on screen.** Talking *about* an AI
advisor is abstract and doesn't convert. Watching one catch a counterfeit does.

## The script formula

**Every Seoul Sister video: demo-led, 20-35 seconds.**

| Beat | Time | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook, hands moving | 0-3s | Confession or paradigm-flip. Motion holds the eye while the claim lands. Identity-framed beats ingredient-framed. |
| 2. **The demo** | 3-20s | **Screen-record the actual thing happening.** This is the conversion mechanism. Do not narrate over it — let it land. |
| 3. The un-co-opted beat | 20-30s | If de-influencing: **offer NO alternative product.** That's the whole differentiator (see below). |
| 4. Name it once | ~30s | "It's called Seoul Sister." Native register. Nothing more. |

### De-influencing is fresh for Bailey specifically

The trend is alive in 2026 but **co-opted** — audiences learned that most "don't buy X" is really
"buy Y instead," i.e. influencing in a wrapper. Yuri telling you to buy less **with no alternative
offered** is the un-co-opted version, and it's the credibility gap most de-influencers cannot fill.

**Hard rule: the video must visibly NOT pivot to a product recommendation.** The moment it does, it
collapses into the pattern viewers have already learned to distrust, and the differentiator is gone.

### Human-talking-about-AI is favored; AI-generated video is not

TikTok penalizes AI-*generated* footage (C2PA Content Credentials auto-detection; static AI avatars
reportedly get far less distribution). It does **not** penalize content *about* AI tools, and
consumer receptivity is high (64% of UK adults used AI to guide a beauty purchase in the last 6
months — Tier A/B).

**Operational rule: never post Yuri-avatar-led video to Bailey's account.** Screen recordings of
the real app inside a human-fronted video are fine and are the point.

### CTA language

⚠️ **"I built this with my dad" is NOT the default closer — it is the ORIGIN-STORY closer.**
(Corrected July 25 2026 after Scott flagged it.) It is true, warm, unfakeable, and satisfies FTC
disclosure in five words. But it carries a real cost in the wrong slot: it can read as *"my dad has
a hobby project,"* it shrinks a 6,000-product database to a family anecdote, and it casts Bailey as
the **daughter** rather than the **co-creator** who drove product decisions and got a whole feature
deleted by calling it garbage.

**Match the closer to what the video just showed:**

| Video type | Closer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Origin story** (SS-5, R-7 Hwahae) | "I built this with my dad." | The video IS the origin. It lands. |
| **Product demo** (SS-1, R-1, R-3) | "It's called Seoul Sister. It reads about fifteen thousand ingredients." | A demo of sophisticated software must not close on a phrase that shrinks it. |
| **Authority / contrarian** (R-6, R-8) | "I co-created this. Six thousand products, and it tells you which four are yours." | Establishes she is the builder, not the helper. |
| **Short / low-key** (R-4, R-10) | "It's called Seoul Sister." | Name alone. Nothing needed. |

**Verified numbers she can say on camera** (live DB, July 25 2026 — re-check before filming, these
grow): **6,051 products · 598 brands · 14,952 ingredients · 230,492 ingredient links · 5,114 price
records.** Round down when speaking ("about six thousand," "around fifteen thousand"). These are
true+specific capability claims, which the video playbook's CLAIMS GUARDRAIL explicitly permits —
unlike "dermatologist-level," which it bans.

**Other CTAs that work:** "seoulsister.com if you want Yuri to look at your routine." · URL simply
visible in the screen recording · pinned comment with the URL · reply-to-comment videos answering
"what app is that?"

**Say the brand name ALOUD.** Not because "link in bio" is penalized — that's unmeasured folklore,
and the phrase is allowed — but because a spoken name survives Duets, reposts and screen-recordings
where the bio is lost, and captures people who search the name instead of clicking (369 existing
Bing AI citations make that a real path).

**Avoid:** price in the hook · "sign up now" · follow/like/share asks (that IS documented engagement
bait) · CTA with no demo behind it (nothing to want) · hard-sell register.

**The safe/unsafe line, stated precisely:**
- ✅ **SAFE — deliver the full value in the video, then mention where it lives as an afterthought.**
- ❌ **UNSAFE — tease and gate.** "The full routine is in my bio." That is withholding value to
  force a click, which is the documented bait pattern.

This happens to align exactly with the honesty ethos — no manufactured curiosity gaps — so there is
no tension between what converts and what the brand allows.

**⚠️ Friction caveat on the Cal AI comparison.** Cal AI is an **iOS app impulse download**; Seoul
Sister is a **$24.99/mo web subscription**. Materially higher friction. The demo-led *mechanism*
transfers; **their conversion rates do not.** Expect worse, and plan for it.

## THE TEST (this is the point of the document)

The literature cannot settle explicit-vs-soft. Bailey's own account can, for free.

**3 arms, 4 videos each, 12 total.** Identical format otherwise — demo-led, 20-35s.

| Arm | CTA |
|---|---|
| **A** | Demo + spoken name-search CTA ("it's called Seoul Sister") |
| **B** | Demo + pinned-comment CTA only, nothing spoken |
| **C** | Demo + no CTA at all (the original playbook's position) |

**The teacher (least-gameable available): `/tt` sessions per 1,000 views.**

```sql
SELECT source, COUNT(*) AS sessions, ROUND(AVG(message_count),1) AS avg_msgs
FROM ss_widget_sessions
WHERE started_at >= '<test start>'
GROUP BY source;
```
Read against GA4 view counts per video. Secondary: widget conversations started, emails captured.

**Baseline is zero `tt_ss` sessions (never any TikTok traffic), so any session is signal.**

**Prediction, recorded in advance so it can be falsified:** A wins, C produces near-zero.
Grade ~4-6 weeks out. Record the outcome here and in `LEAD-GEN-LEARNINGS-LOG.md` whichever way it
goes — a falsified prediction is the point of writing it down.

## Realistic expectations (set these before starting)

Follower count barely gates reach — distribution is per-video via FYP, so 1,200 followers is not the
constraint. But the funnel is brutal (Tier B benchmarks):

> **10,000-view video ≈ 30-150 site sessions ≈ 0-2 registrations, 0-1 paid.**
> **Ordinary 500-view video ≈ 0.**

**Signups arrive in lumps from occasional outlier videos, not as a steady drip.** Judge on outlier
economics, never per-video averages. Cal AI's edge was volume — near-daily posting so the power law
had chances to hit.

**For the next 60 days the success metric is `tt` sessions and Yuri conversations started, NOT paid
subs.** One paid subscriber from TikTok is a genuine win. Several weeks of zero is normal and is not
evidence of failure.

## Cadence

~10 videos/week, all inside ONE niche (skincare & beauty — her bio already says it). Posting across
3+ unrelated topics costs roughly 45% reach.

| Format | /wk | Shop tag? |
|---|---|---|
| Skincare value (SS often invisible) | ~4 | No |
| Product content (TikTok Shop affiliate) | ~3 | **Yes — zero SS mention** |
| Founder / build-in-public | ~2 | No |
| Life / personality | ~1 | No |

**The lane rule is absolute:** a Shop-tagged video must never mention Seoul Sister or point
off-platform. TikTok Shop penalizes "sending traffic off app" in shop-tagged content including
implied references; 24 violation points in 90 days = ban. Protect the account — it is the
distribution.

**FTC:** Bailey is an owner-promoter. Material connection disclosed in-video AND above the caption
fold. Not a hashtag. "I co-created this app" / "I built this with my dad" satisfies it and is also
the best-performing framing.

caught up.
