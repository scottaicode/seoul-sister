# LGAAS Work Order: blog posts quote review figures nobody collected

**Date:** November 14 2026
**Status:** OPEN
**Audience:** any AI session working in the LGAAS repo.

## What is wrong

38 sentences in 18 published Seoul Sister posts quote a star rating or a review count
("4.6 stars across roughly 5,800 reviews") for a product. Every one of those figures matches,
exactly, a number typed into the February 2026 launch seed files. None came from a retailer or
from members. Seoul Sister removed the figures from its catalog on Nov 14 2026 (v11.61.11), so the
posts now cite numbers that exist nowhere.

One claim was checked and is real, and stays: Etude Soonjung pH 5.5 Foam Cleanser, 4.8 stars
across roughly 215 reviews (an Olive Young figure).

## What to do

1. For each sentence below, remove the parenthetical or clause that states the rating or the
   review count. Do not replace it with another number or with "highly reviewed", "top-rated",
   "most-reviewed" or any other popularity claim: there is no measurement behind one.
2. Read the sentence after the cut. Where the figure was the reason given for the pick
   ("the highest-reviewed salicylic pad"), give the real reason (the formula) or drop the claim.
3. Write the old and new sentence pairs to a replacements file and second-model review them,
   as for the first-person-claims fix, so both copies end up with the same text. Seoul Sister
   applies the same file to its published posts.
4. Check the generator: it was handed catalog ratings and quoted them. Seeded products now come
   back with no rating, so new drafts should not repeat this, but confirm on one fresh draft.

## Not covered

This list comes from one sentence shape, "(… roughly N reviews)". Other wordings of a review
count or star rating were not searched. Sweep each post for them while editing.
