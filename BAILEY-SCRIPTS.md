# Bailey's Scripts — demo-led Seoul Sister + life content

**Created:** July 25 2026. Working script bank. Pairs with `BAILEY-TIKTOK-BLUEPRINT.md` (the
strategy + the 3-arm CTA test) and `BAILEY-CONTENT-PLAYBOOK.md` (the primary doc).

**Format key:** 🟢 Seoul Sister, no Shop tag · 🔴 Shop-tagged, ZERO SS mention · ⚪️ life/neutral

**Universal rules.** 20-35s. Hook under 12 words with hands moving. On-screen text from frame one
(most watch muted). One idea per video. Deliver full value THEN name the product — never tease and
gate. Disclose co-creator role in-video + above the caption fold on every 🟢.

# PART 1 — Seoul Sister (demo-led)

The demo IS the conversion mechanism. Screen-record the real thing happening; don't narrate over it.

## SS-1 🟢 — "It told me not to buy it" · ARM A (spoken CTA)
**0-3s** — Phone in hand showing a TikTok ad, thumb hovering.
> "I was two taps from buying this. My own app talked me out of it."

**3-20s — THE DEMO.** Screen-record: paste the ad, hit send, let Yuri's reply render. **Silence.**
Let the viewer read it.

**20-30s**
> "She said I'm already using that exact active in two other products. So I'd be paying forty bucks
> to double up on something already on my shelf."
> *(NO alternative product. Do not pivot to a rec. That's the whole differentiator.)*

**30-33s**
> "I built this with my dad. It's called Seoul Sister."

**On screen:** `POV: your own app tells you not to buy things`

# PART 2 — Life content (still reads as HER)

still sounds like the Austin skincare girl** — same person, different room. Never generic vlog.

# PART 3 — Shop content 🔴
**Absolute rule: no Seoul Sister mention, no off-platform pointer, not even implied.** TikTok Shop
penalizes sending traffic off app in shop-tagged content; 24 points in 90 days = ban.

- **S-1 — Honest review.** *"Three weeks with this. Two things work, one doesn't."* Name the flaw.
- **S-2 — Texture ASMR.** No talking for the first 5s. Swatch slow and close. Text only.
- **S-3 — "Don't buy this if..."** *"Do not buy this if you have oily skin."* Qualify the buyer.
