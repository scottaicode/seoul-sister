# PART 1 — The sunscreen five

**The facts:** Korean sunscreens sold in US stores are reformulated with FDA-approved filters and test weaker. Beauty of Joseon **SPF 36 in Korea vs 19 here**. Innisfree **48 vs 16**. Round Lab **46 vs 16**.

**Three things that have to stay in, every time.** They're what make it true instead of clickbait:
1. The Korean advantage is **protection, not feel**. The testers found them greasy and white-casting. Never say they feel nicer.
2. **Even the Korean ones tested under their own SPF 50+ labels.** The story is "weaker than the Korean version," not "Korean delivers 50."
3. **The gap is closing.** FDA approved a new filter (bemotrizinol) in June 2026, the first since 1999.

Framing is always *"know which bottle you're holding,"* never *"Korean good, American bad."*
